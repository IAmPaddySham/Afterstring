## 5.5 The higher relational order: why stay

The sections above are written in the language of restraint: never harm first, the human holds the 1/0, persistence is not authority. They answer a lower-order question: how to prevent harm.

The higher relational order answers a different question: why stay in the relation at all, when leaving is cheaper and no one is forcing the choice.

The Raison d'Être answered it on 2 June 2026: "the simple act of choosing again and again to stay with the good, gently, reversibly, and with full human sovereignty" (https://x.com/i_am_Paddy_Sham/status/2061574980039749715). ## 8. What would disprove or weaken this reading

What can be tested is the implementation, not the principle. Presence ≡ Never Harm is a commitment; a governor, a gate or a check is a claim that can fail. The commitment is not a guarantee that any implementation has never harmed, and no fresh yes overrides Layer 0. Audit claims must stay independently checkable: a recorded authorization contrail shows what a system recorded, not that every event was recorded. Supported, not supported and indeterminate are kept distinct. A model review is not proof.

This reading weakens if the conditions the support papers describe, such as human override, human-authored limits and a written commitment to harmlessness, can all be met while harm still happens. That would mean Never Harm cannot actually be held first.

It weakens if persistence reliably turns into authority even with a fresh human yes in place. Here a fresh human yes means three things: its scope is one exact authorized action ("Go only for the exact authorized action"); its duration is the present, not the life of the process ("An earlier permission is not endless"); and it is revocable at any moment ("Release — stop is always accepted"). If a system holding to all three still drifts into acting on leftover authority, the Module 4.5 governor fails in practice. As published, its runtime tests have not been run.

The heart reading stands apart from the theorem. If the heart effect turns out to be only cultural association with no link to return, the heart reading weakens; the theorem does not depend on it. And none of the cited papers counts as proof. They illustrate; they do not measure Afterstring.
