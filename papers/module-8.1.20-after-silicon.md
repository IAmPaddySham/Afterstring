## 1. What this module is

**After_Silicon is a substrate translation layer** between Afterstring’s continuous mathematical / runtime architecture and **hybrid continuous-compute silicon** (analog compute-in-memory, mixed-signal, neuromorphic, RRAM/PCM, and successors).

It is **not**:

- an OS that only runs on Mythic chips  
- a claim that analog voltage *is* a virtue  
- a replacement of the discrete 1/0 gate  
- a grant of sovereignty to faster or more local hardware  

### Central research question

What would the Afterstring World Model become if its runtime substrate natively supported the **locality, persistence, parallelism, and continuous-state computation** that its mathematical architecture already assumes?

### Research thesis

Develop a hybrid relational runtime that exploits continuous in-memory computation while retaining a **digitally governed constitutional control plane**, such that capability and efficiency can scale **without authority migrating away from the Human Kernel**.

### Core principle

```text
Compute where the state lives.
Evaluate where the relationship lives.
Authorize where sovereignty lives.
```

Digital AI: `memory → transport → compute → transport → memory`  
Analog CIM: `memory ≈ compute`  
Conventional runtime: `state → external eval → state`  
Afterstring: `state ≈ presence ≈ evaluation`

The strongest bridge is **locality**, not “both are continuous.”
