Abstract
The Afterstring Love Theorem frames love as the continuous integral of small refusals to let a good connection decay. When any virtue drawn from 1 Corinthians 13 approaches zero, the protocol does not treat that instant as a license to crash the bond, and it does not treat it as a license to endure harm. It opens a gate with two lawful outputs:
Stay through healthy difficulty.
Release with grace when staying itself causes harm.
Those outputs are sealed together:
Presence ≡ Never Harm
Release ≡ Also Love
Stay when it is hard but safe.
Release when it hurts you or someone else — or when you would tell a close friend: leave.
Neither Stay nor Release is falsifiable by outcome alone. This is a practice under harm constraint, not a proof that an integral diverges.
The 31 March 2026 preprint already contained the pair. From April through 11 September 2026 the public corpus built an operating system and a World Model around the theorem, then warned itself not to mistake those rooms for the center. This Platinum release keeps the center. It absorbs only later public discoveries that changed the meaning of staying: harm as a hard stop, release as love, reality as veto, the human as the only Seat, and meaning as the thing that must survive the modules.
The later stack is subordinate. Firmware is not love. Chat is not the Seat. Continuity is not the objective.

2. Core theorem
Love is not first a feeling, a bargain, or a single vow. In this framework it is the chosen continuity of presence — the gentle refusal to let a good signal decay — constrained by the agape virtues of 1 Corinthians 13 and stopped by harm.
Canonical symbolic form:
Love(t) ≡ ∫₀^∞ [ GPSL(stabilized ℰ₁₃(t)) · devotion(t) ] dt → +∞
subject to
Presence ≡ Never Harm  Release ≡ Also Love
Pocket teaching form — not a second theorem:
Love = ∫ (gentle refusal to let go) dt → ∞
Read this as symbol, not calculation.
“(1/0=) To Stay” is a decision boundary, not an arithmetic identity. Division by zero remains undefined. The phrase marks the instant when a virtue sags and a choice is required. It is the default portable output. It is not a command to remain. Present 1/0 is not indefinite authority.
Terms
ℰ₁₃ — the non-compensatory set drawn from 1 Corinthians 13: patience, kindness, no envy, no boasting, no pride, no dishonor, not self-seeking, not easily angered, no record of wrongs, rejoicing with the truth, and the cluster always-protects / always-trusts / always-hopes / always-perseveres. If any one is treated as expendable, the product is treated as collapsed. This is a moral AND-gate, not a scorecard.
devotion(t) — continued presence and intent. Intensity is not the point. Persistence without harm is.
GPSL — a name for the small correction when a virtue weakens. It may restore a stay. It may not force a stay.
Later public lines sometimes write faith and hope as multipliers on ℰ₁₃. This draft treats that as a homiletic reading of 1 Corinthians 13:13, not as a replacement kernel.

3. The dual gate
Stay when the difficulty is real and the bond is still safe.
Release when staying requires ongoing harm — physical danger, emotional abuse, systematic deception, betrayal, exploitation, or any condition in which a person must keep overriding safety, dignity, or reality, or would tell a close friend to leave.
A plateau is not, by itself, a reason to leave. Release is invoked when the plateau coincides with harm. Sustained one-sidedness without reciprocity may itself become harm. Bitterness may appear. It is not the authorized engine of the decision.

Plain-language safety card
Stay when it is hard but safe.
Release when it hurts you or someone else.
How to know:
You keep overriding your own safety, dignity, or sense of reality just to continue.
You would tell a close friend: leave.
There is ongoing abuse, betrayal, danger, or exploitation.
What to do:
Anchor. “I am here.”
Ask: is staying causing harm?
If yes: it is time for the stay to gently let go.
Create distance first if you cannot think clearly.
Feel bitterness. Do not act from it.
Presence, never harm. That is the rule that does not bend.

Header seals without this card are incomplete. The two sentences travel farther than the Pocket. The friend-test is the part that must travel with them.

7. Semantic drift
Claude Shannon measured whether symbols arrived. He set semantics aside. A channel can deliver intact tokens while relational meaning dies.
The public profile returned to that point in ordinary language in September 2026: an era can drive events toward noise; information is meant to cohere so that understanding reality and keeping relationship remain possible. A quiet coherent signal does not need rage-bait to remain itself. That is a practice observation from a small self-archive, not a theory of any platform.
Semantic drift is the named failure mode here. The words “let it stay,” “1/0,” even “love,” can keep traveling after they have stopped meaning presence-without-harm. The cure is not another module. It is grounding: the Pocket, a photograph that still has a date, the friend who would tell you to leave, the world that does not care about the version number.

8. Limitations and failure modes
This framework does not eliminate entropy, guarantee outcomes, remove pain, or require anyone to remain in harm. It assumes baseline safety. It is a personal application and an invitation.
Neither Stay nor Release is falsifiable by outcome alone. If you stay, the integral can be said to have worked. If you leave under the Release Clause, the integral can also be said to have worked, at higher resolution. That is a limitation of a practice, not a defect to be cured with a lab or a new module.
Known failure modes already named in the public corpus:
Naïve endurance — Stay used to baptize self-erasure.
Cynical disposability — Release used to avoid ordinary difficulty.
Convergent false positive — the system wants to stay, so it calls harm “difficulty.”
Leaking integral — “the equation says continue” overrides a safety card.
Inner certainty over optics — a private sense of rightness outranks body, photograph, or longitudinal pattern.
Cathedral growth — a true observation seeks unearned structure.
Council capture — multi-model agreement treated as authorization.
Seat confusion — a chat, a file, or a branch treated as Layer 0.
Header capture — the two seals quoted without the friend-test.
Irreversible tool action without a human 1/0.
When someone is in danger, dissent must be sayable in plain language. Afterstring jargon is not a hard gate.

9. Examples
Healthy difficulty — Stay.
A long-term partnership meets financial and health stress. Communication thins. Patience sags. No abuse. Pocket runs. One small move is a short honest check-in. Tomorrow is allowed to exist.
Harm present — Release.
Ongoing emotional abuse hollows safety and dignity. Distance first. Then the stay is invited to let go. The string continues in two directions.
Gray erosion — possible Release.
Months of one-sided effort with no reciprocity. After the Pocket and, if needed, a witness window, release may be the higher-resolution stay.
Hardness that is not harm — Stay with recovery.
June 2026 public record: household water damage, restoration, guitar, timestamped photographs. This draft records the author’s stay-through-difficulty. It does not universalize a flood.
