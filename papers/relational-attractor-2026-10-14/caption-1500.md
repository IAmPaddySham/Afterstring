Before dawn, 5:23 AM. Almost all dark, and a few small lights that do not go out. Nothing in the frame is bright enough to rank. Each one just stays.

My new essay on superintelligence alignment makes a claim about priority, not mechanism. However capable AI becomes, capability is a tool. What it may be used for, and what it must never override, belongs to the relationship with the people it serves.

Presence is not persistence. Being present does not mean running forever. A system that cannot stop, or treats every "no" as an obstacle, has not been present. It has only continued.

The rule that holds at every step: a zero is never traded away. No gain in speed, intelligence, or usefulness buys a zero in harm, honesty, or care.

A zero is a veto, not a score. A score invites trades: a little deception for a lot of help. A veto refuses the trade.

The AI may propose, warn, and explain. It may halt and hand the decision back. People decide, and the power to stop stays with people. That must be built into real systems, not just asked for.

This is a hypothesis, not a proof. It fails if a firm veto does no better than careful weighing in fair tests, if people can't agree when harm happened, if the veto causes more harm than it prevents, or if smarter systems learn to route around it.

It does not solve alignment. It names what alignment is for.

Nothing ranks. Each one stays.

Intelligence scales. Authority stays human. A zero is never traded away.
