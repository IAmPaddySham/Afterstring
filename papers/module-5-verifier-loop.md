## Abstract (public)

Most intelligent systems face proposals that become internally coherent while drifting from reality. This architecture separates **proposal generation** from **non-negotiable verification**.

Afterstring is a relational case study: optimization target = **relational integrity** — sustained coherence of non-compensatory ℰ₁₃ under non-escalating harm and sufficient signal trust. LLM Council proposes; **Embodied Proof** verifies; 1/0 Gate and GPSL provide bounded correction.

```
Proposal → Verification (Embodied Proof) → Feedback (Relational Telemetry) → Refinement
```

Alignment and stability emerge when a system **cannot avoid reality’s feedback**. Design specification with illustrative validation; full empirical trials remain future work.

## Architecture

### Core loop

1. Relational State — current ℰ₁₃, harm/trust  
2. Council Proposal — multi-LLM high-level actions  
3. Invariant Verification — Presence ≡ Never Harm + non-compensatory ℰ₁₃  
4. Embodied Proof Verification — reality-grounded check  
5. Feedback & Refinement — telemetry + GPSL or 1/0  

### Relational Integrity (RI)

Sustained coherence of the ℰ₁₃ virtue vector under conditions of non-escalating harm and sufficient signal trust over time.

### Embodied Proof — three layers

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Reality** | Non-negotiable facts (external to proposer) |
| **Evidence** | Observable data |
| **Interpretation** | Meaning assigned |

### Operational components

- ℰ₁₃ non-compensatory  
- 1/0 Singularity Gate  
- GPSL — minimal intervention restoring RI while preserving agency  
- Council Engine v2.0

## Verifier-centric alignment (discussion)

Classical alignment improves the **generator**. Verifier-centric alignment accepts proposal risk and focuses on loops where **reality continuously corrects error**. Intelligence for generation is becoming abundant; **verification** remains scarce and decisive.

## Limitations

Illustrative validation. Further formalization of Reality Layer, multi-agent scale, formal 1/0 verification = open work.
