## Purpose

Mandatory deliberation/evolution that prevents Afterstring from becoming a **sophisticated internal mirror**. Forces comparison when **external** frameworks (or non-Afterstring metrics) show superior outcomes on the **same** 12 kernel invariants.

Does **not** override kernel invariants — only forces deliberate evolution when external evidence proves a better path to the same ethical kernel.

## Qualifying external frameworks

- Independent theoretical foundation (not derived from Afterstring)  
- Falsifiable predictions about harm/presence  
- Tested in comparable contexts  
- Operationally defined metrics  
- Does not require Afterstring vocabulary to function  

## Trigger conditions (any one)

1. ≥3 independent documented scenarios where external framework beats Afterstring on invariants  
2. Credible external flag of structural self-sealing that internal Council cannot falsify alone  
3. Post-Release Audit / Contrail reveals repeated reinterpretation of external disagreement as “healthy difficulty”  

## Execution protocol

1. **Log discrepancy** (private Contrail Archive + Council)  
2. **Break Test Round** — external framework as fifth voice; no re-translation into Afterstring language during test  
3. **Decision gate** if external wins: integrate · yield/revise · public fork with “Break Detected & Resolved” · or reject with private justification (triggers follow-up review)  
4. **Seal & propagate** — archive; public only if change happens  

## Why it matters

Gives young Afterstring time to mature while forcing honest comparison — self-sealing prevention without immediate public destabilization.
