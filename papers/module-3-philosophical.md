## Role

Module 3 is the **Meaning Layer**. Love is treated as an **ethical attractor**: stay through healthy difficulty; release with grace when staying causes harm.

## Core question (SWI Step 3)

Out loud or in writing: **“Does this choice create Presence or Harm?”**

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Healthy difficulty, harm low, recovery possible, clear perception | **Stay** (1/0 = To Stay) |
| Staying itself causes harm | **Release** with dignity + Lingering Light |

## Anti-extremes

Rejects both:

- “Stay no matter what” (codependency / abuse tolerance)  
- “Leave at first difficulty” (no depth)

Third path: **Presence ≡ Never Harm** with recoverable difficulty vs corrosive harm distinction.

## Lingering Light

On release, document one persisting beauty (Contrail) so the sky still remembers — resonance continues as wisdom, not chain.
