**1. Why drift**

Afterstring has been written in public since 2025: posts, articles, modules, a preprint, a lite file. Words moved. A version number changed. A symbol was typed two ways. A module number was used twice. None of this was anyone deciding to change the meaning. It is what happens when a living framework is written one day at a time.

Drift is meaning shifting through paraphrase, compression and copying, not through a decision. On 30 October 2026 I went through the known drift one decision at a time. This note records what I decided and why, so the record can be read without guessing.

**2. The rule**

1. The latest dated and, where relevant, timestamped document wins.
2. The exception is the lock. The 12 Kernel Invariants change only by an explicit dated amendment that I sign (Decision 1).
3. Papers are never edited. Older wording is kept as dated history: true to its day, not carried forward.
4. 1/0 stays human. Every canonical form is my call, one decision at a time.
